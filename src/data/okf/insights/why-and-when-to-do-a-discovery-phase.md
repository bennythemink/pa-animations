# Summary
A Discovery phase gathers context, clarifies objectives, and identifies risks before a project starts. For small or already well-documented projects, a short scoping session is enough — a full Discovery phase is only necessary when the project is large or complex, and teams that want to jump straight into design or development without it should be treated with caution.
