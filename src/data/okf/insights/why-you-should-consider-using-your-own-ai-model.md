# Summary
Open-source AI models have closed the gap with proprietary frontier models and can be self-hosted, giving businesses control over version, uptime, cost, and data instead of depending on a third-party provider's decisions. That dependency risk became concrete in June 2026, when the US government forced Anthropic to pull its newly launched Fable 5 and Mythos 5 models offline within 90 minutes over a national security concern, taking any product built on them dark without warning. Self-hosting isn't right for every use case, since frontier capability still sits with proprietary providers, but for business-critical applications it's worth weighing that control against the marginal performance gap.
