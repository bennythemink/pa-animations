# Summary
Non-technical clients evaluating a proposed tech solution should ideally get an independent technical review before committing. If that isn't possible, key questions to ask include: is a custom build actually justified over an off-the-shelf platform, how easy is it to export your data (vendor lock-in), should a mobile app be native or cross-platform, who owns the source code, could another team pick up the work later, and who is responsible for maintenance after launch. A small upfront investment in review is almost always cheaper than discovering the wrong technical call was made once a project is underway.
