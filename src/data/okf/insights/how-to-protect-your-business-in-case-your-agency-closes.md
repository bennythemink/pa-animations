# Summary
Pixel Agency's founders previously worked at an agency that collapsed due to mismanagement, and learned firsthand what separated clients who came through fine from those who didn't. The key protections: own or have direct access to your hosting (with regular backups), your app store credentials, your source code, your content, and your domain, and know who holds your plugin licences, plus have a real human contact inside the agency, not just a support ticket system, for early warning. None of this requires technical knowledge, they're just questions any client can ask their agency at any time.
