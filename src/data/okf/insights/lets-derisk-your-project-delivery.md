# Summary
Projects typically fail in one of two ways: the idea was never properly validated, or the delivery process itself falls apart. The fix for the first is market research and an MVP rather than building the whole solution at once; the fix for the second is a proper staged delivery process, Discovery, Design, Development, Beta/UAT, Deployment, and Post-launch support, with a client sign-off gate at each stage. Other de-risking tactics include building a vertical slice early, weekly progress updates, staged payments tied to milestones, and clear IP ownership; a provider that skips or obscures these steps, or resists an independent technical review, is a red flag.
