# Summary
No, the current AI business model is not sustainable. No major provider has demonstrated profitability; training and inference costs remain enormous, consumer subscriptions are heavily subsidised, and data centre infrastructure is being cancelled, blocked, or delayed at scale due to power and cost constraints. Becoming sustainable likely requires a hardware breakthrough plus major inference efficiency gains, neither guaranteed, and in the meantime the providers that survive will be those that close the multi-billion-dollar gap between what it costs to serve a request and what customers are willing to pay.
