# Summary
Google's new Open Knowledge Format (OKF, a v0.1 draft) stores company knowledge as plain markdown files with YAML metadata, readable without tooling, versionable in git, and portable between systems, unlike knowledge trapped in Notion, Confluence, or an unmaintained wiki. Its real value is what it implies: knowledge should be actively designed (structured, tagged, cross-linked, timestamped) rather than just accumulated, which is also the groundwork RAG systems like Pixel Assist need to retrieve well. OKF isn't a silver bullet though, a well-structured knowledge base that nobody maintains will still go stale just as fast as a poorly structured one.
