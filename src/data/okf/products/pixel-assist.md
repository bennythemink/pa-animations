# Overview

Pixel Assist is a managed, AI-powered knowledge assistant for websites. It learns a client's content, products, documentation, specifications, policies, and FAQs, and uses it to answer customer questions instantly through a chat-like interface, rather than users digging through pages, PDFs, or contacting support.

# How It Works

1. Ingest your knowledge - collect website content, documents, and resources.
2. Train the assistant - the system learns your terminology, products, and processes using modern AI techniques.
3. Deploy to your site - the assistant is integrated directly into your website experience.
4. Continuously report & improve - monthly insights reports, performance monitoring, and ongoing optimisation.

# Features

# Benefits

- **Improve customer experience** - Give people instant answers without forcing them to search or contact support.
- **Speed up decisions** - Help customers, buyers, and stakeholders choose faster with clear, accurate information.
- **Convert more visitors** - When a website actively helps people find what they need, they're more likely to stay, engage, and convert.
- **Reduce repetitive enquiries** - Free the support team from answering the same questions again and again.
- **Increase consistency** - Ensure everyone receives the same, correct information every time.
- **Scale support without hiring** - Serve more people without increasing headcount.
- **Differentiate your brand** - Be known as the easiest organisation to work with.

# Who It's For

Ideal for organisations with large knowledge bases, detailed or technical products, compliance or policy documentation, complex services, or frequent support questions. Common examples include manufacturers and wholesalers, education and training providers, professional services firms, SaaS and technology platforms, and internal knowledge bases and portals. If a team repeatedly answers the same questions, it is likely a fit.
